Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Speed Ledger doesn't use accounts or sign-in. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Will I lose my data if I get a new phone?
If you've turned on iCloud backup in Settings, your data will carry over automatically. You can also export your history as a CSV file from Settings at any time and import it on a new device.

Can I use this without following the HiiTS program?
The app is built around that training structure, but you're free to log sets and sessions at your own pace regardless.

How do I turn off notifications?
Go to Settings → Session reminders and turn the toggle off, or manage notification permissions directly in iOS Settings.

What does the "tip" option do?
It's a completely optional way to support ongoing development. It doesn't unlock anything — the full app is free either way.

Found a bug?

Email me with what happened and, if you can, what device and iOS version you're on — that makes it much easier to track down.
