Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Month Day, Year]
In short: Speed Ledger doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to any third party. Everything you log stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up to your own iCloud account, export it yourself, or share it yourself.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Speed Ledger does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information in order to function. We do not operate any servers that receive, store, or process your training data. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking tools of any kind.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

The app stores the information you choose to enter directly on your device, including:

  • Your training sessions, sets, and swing-speed readings
  • Your baseline numbers and goal speeds
  • Any notes you add to a session or set
  • Your name, if you choose to enter one, and your training-day preferences

This information is stored locally using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. We never see it, and we have no way to access it.

3. iCloud Backup (Optional)

If you enable iCloud backup in Settings, your data may be backed up to your own personal iCloud account, using Apple's infrastructure. This is governed by Apple's own privacy practices, not ours. We do not have access to your iCloud account or the data stored there. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

4. Exporting and Importing Your Data

The app lets you export your training history as a CSV file and share it using the iOS share sheet (for example, via email or Files), and lets you import a CSV file from this app or a compatible one. These actions happen entirely on your device and are initiated only when you choose to use them. We are not involved in, and cannot see, any file you export or import.

5. Notifications

If you opt in to session reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device using Apple's notification framework. These are not sent from a server — no data about your training leaves your phone in order to generate them. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

6. In-App Purchases (Tips)

Speed Ledger offers optional, one-time tips to support development. These purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

7. Third-Party Links

Parts of the app (such as Tips & Tricks) may link to third-party content, including videos we did not create. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party site or service you visit through such a link.

8. Children's Privacy

Speed Ledger does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all. The app is not directed at children.

9. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device (and, optionally, your own iCloud account), its security is governed by the security of your device and your Apple ID, not by us.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
